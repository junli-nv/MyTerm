# MyTerm 3.1.10

## 简体中文

- 修复更改标签名称后标题未立即刷新的问题，名称与标签宽度同步更新。右键或 Control+单击会先选中目标标签再打开菜单，移除多余的“选中会话”菜单项。
- 增加实际重命名弹窗的保存、重复修改、取消、切换与排序后标题显示检查，以及未选中标签的右键选择检查。

## English

- Fix tab titles failing to refresh after renaming, with matching tab width updates. Right-click or Control-click selects the target tab before opening its menu; remove the redundant Select Session item.
- Add real rename-dialog checks for saving, repeated edits, cancellation, title display after switching/reordering, and right-click selection of inactive tabs.
