# MyTerm 3.4.1

## 简体中文

- SSH 编辑器移除重复的“选择已有跳板配置”入口，保留逐级选择、更换和跳板机管理。
- 已保存的跳板链、代理、共享引用和密码保留；逐级添加不会整套替换路径或代理。
- 同步中英文使用文档与开发上下文，验证共享跳板选择、凭据复用和编辑器布局。

## English

- Remove the redundant whole-route saved jump configuration menu from the SSH editor; retain individual hop selection, replacement and jump management.
- Preserve saved jump chains, proxies, shared references and passwords; adding a hop does not replace the route or proxy.
- Update bilingual usage and development context and validate shared hop selection, credential reuse and editor layout.
