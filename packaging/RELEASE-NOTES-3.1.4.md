# MyTerm 3.1.4

## 简体中文

- 跳板机可提前填写密码，留空保留已有密码或连接时询问。
- 多个会话使用同一路径时共享跳板机加密密码，密码管理显示关联会话。
- 从已保存会话选择并复制完整跳转路径及网络代理，支持多级跳板。

## English

- Enter jump passwords in advance, or leave blank to reuse saved credentials or ask when connecting.
- Sessions sharing a route reuse encrypted jump passwords; password management lists their references.
- Select and copy saved multi-hop routes and network proxies from existing sessions.
