# MyTerm 3.2.1

## 简体中文

修复混合上下、左右分屏时的尺寸变化。新分屏只等分当前区域，其他区域和手动调整的分隔比例保持不变；窗口缩放按现有比例分配空间。分隔比例在当前运行期间保留，不写入保存的会话。

## English

Fix sizing when nesting horizontal and vertical splits. A new split halves only its selected region; other panes and manually adjusted proportions remain unchanged. Window resizing retains existing proportions. Proportions are retained during the current run, not persisted in saved sessions.
