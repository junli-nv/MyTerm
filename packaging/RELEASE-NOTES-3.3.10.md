# MyTerm 3.3.10

## 简体中文

- 终端历史搜索：⌘F 打开，回车/Shift+回车或 ⌘G/⌘⇧G 导航，支持大小写、整词与正则匹配、中文和软折行、无匹配提示及 Esc 退出。仅搜索保留的终端缓冲，不启用日志、不向远端发送文本，持续输出不触发自动扫描。
- 标签颜色与环境标识：SSH 编辑器、标签右键及分屏菜单可设置颜色和最多 24 字的环境文字；显示于标签和分屏标题。
- 保存的 SSH 配置同步更新打开标签；连接复制、保存会话和 MyTerm JSON/偏好备份保留标识，不影响 SSH 参数与凭据。
- 更新中英文文档、界面翻译和回归检查。

## English

- Terminal history search: ⌘F opens search; Return/Shift+Return and ⌘G/⌘⇧G navigate. Supports case-sensitive/whole-word/regex matching, Chinese and soft wraps, no-match feedback and Esc to close. Searches retained terminal buffers without enabling logs or sending text remotely; ongoing output does not rescan.
- Tab colors and environment labels: configure colors and labels up to 24 characters in SSH configuration, tab context menus and split-pane menus; displayed in tabs and pane headers.
- Saved SSH configurations update open tabs; duplicated connections, saved sessions and MyTerm JSON/preferences backups retain metadata without changing SSH arguments or credentials.
- Matching Chinese/English documentation, localization and regression checks.
